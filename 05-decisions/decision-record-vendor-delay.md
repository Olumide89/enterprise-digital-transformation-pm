# Decision Record — Vendor Delay Response

**Decision ID:** DEC-001  
**Date:** Month 7  
**Decision owner:** Steering committee  
**Prepared by:** Project Manager

## 1. Situation

The implementation vendor has notified the project of a six-week delay in delivering configuration and integration work. Initial assessment indicates that the delay affects activities on the critical path and may postpone user acceptance testing and go-live.

## 2. Impact assessment

| Area | Expected impact without action |
|---|---|
| Schedule | Go-live delayed by up to six weeks |
| Cost | Additional vendor support, extended internal team costs and potential penalty exposure |
| Scope | No immediate scope reduction proposed |
| Quality | Risk of compressed testing if go-live date is retained |
| Stakeholders | Reduced confidence among business owners and operational teams |

## 3. Options considered

| Option | Description | Advantages | Disadvantages | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Accept delay | Move go-live by six weeks | Protects testing quality; lowest delivery risk | Delays benefits; may affect business commitments | Not preferred |
| B. Add vendor resources | Increase vendor capacity and run data-migration preparation in parallel | Recovers most of the schedule; preserves scope | Additional cost; onboarding and coordination risk | **Preferred** |
| C. Reduce scope | Defer lower-priority reports and non-critical workflows | Protects schedule and budget | Reduces immediate business value; requires stakeholder agreement | Possible fallback |
| D. Compress testing | Retain original go-live and shorten testing | Avoids visible delay | High quality, adoption and operational risk | Not recommended |

## 4. Recommendation

Adopt **Option B**: add vendor resources, parallelize data-migration preparation, and protect the user-acceptance-testing window. Retain Option C as a contingency if recovery progress is insufficient after two weeks.

## 5. Decision

The steering committee approves Option B, subject to a revised cost estimate and weekly recovery-plan reporting. The project manager will update the schedule baseline, cost baseline, RAID log and change request log once the revised plan is approved.

## 6. Follow-up actions

- Update the milestone schedule and critical-path analysis.
- Raise a formal change request for additional vendor cost.
- Confirm additional vendor resource availability within five working days.
- Report recovery progress weekly to the steering committee.
- Reassess the recovery plan after two weeks.
