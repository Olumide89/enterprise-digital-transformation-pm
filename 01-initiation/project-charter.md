# Project Charter — BerlinRetail GmbH Digital Transformation

**Project name:** Enterprise Digital Transformation Project  
**Simulation scenario** | **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 31 October 2026

## 1. Project purpose

Replace fragmented spreadsheets and legacy workflows with a cloud-based ERP and workflow platform to improve process efficiency, data quality, reporting and decision-making.

## 2. Objectives

1. Implement the cloud ERP and workflow platform within 12 months.
2. Deliver the implementation within the approved €1.2 million budget.
3. Migrate and validate critical master data.
4. Standardize priority operational workflows.
5. Train affected users and achieve measurable adoption.
6. Establish a sustainable support and governance model after go-live.

## 3. Scope

### In scope

- Cloud ERP and workflow-platform implementation.
- Core process design for finance, operations, procurement and reporting.
- Data migration, validation and reconciliation.
- Integration with priority existing systems.
- User acceptance testing.
- Training, communications and change management.
- Go-live and hypercare support.

### Out of scope

- Replacement of all legacy systems.
- Custom development beyond approved integrations.
- Organization-wide business-process re-engineering.
- Long-term managed-services contract negotiation.

## 4. Success criteria

| Criterion | Target |
|---|---|
| Schedule | Go-live within the approved 12-month baseline, subject to approved change control |
| Budget | Complete within the approved €1.2 million cost baseline |
| Data quality | Critical master-data records validated before go-live |
| Adoption | Core users trained and actively using priority workflows after go-live |
| Benefits | Reporting cycle time reduced by 40% within six months of go-live |

## 6. Governance

- **Steering committee:** Monthly strategic decisions, budget changes and major risks.
- **Project board:** Bi-weekly delivery progress, issues and dependencies.
- **Working groups:** Weekly functional and technical workstream coordination.

## 8. Assumptions

- Business key users are available for workshops, testing and training.
- The vendor can mobilize according to the agreed schedule.
- Critical legacy data can be extracted and cleansed to the required standard.
- Existing systems can be integrated through supported interfaces.

## 9. Constraints

- Fixed €1.2 million budget.
- Twelve-month delivery window.
- Business operations must continue during implementation.
- Data-privacy and internal control requirements must be maintained.
