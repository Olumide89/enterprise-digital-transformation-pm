# Business Case — BerlinRetail GmbH Digital Transformation

**Status:** Draft | **Simulation scenario** | **Prepared by:** Olumide Adeniji

## 1. Executive summary

BerlinRetail GmbH currently relies on fragmented spreadsheets, manual approvals and disconnected legacy workflows. This creates duplicated data entry, delayed reporting, weak auditability and limited visibility across departments.

This business case recommends a 12-month, €1.2 million programme to implement a cloud-based ERP and workflow platform. The initiative is expected to improve data quality, standardize core processes, reduce manual effort and provide management with timely operational insight.

## 2. Business problem

- Customer, inventory, finance and operational data are held in separate spreadsheets and legacy systems.
- Approvals depend on manual handoffs, creating delays and inconsistent controls.
- Reporting is time-consuming and often inconsistent between departments.
- Management lacks a single source of truth for operational and financial performance.

## 3. Proposed solution

Implement an integrated cloud ERP and workflow platform covering core operational processes, approvals, reporting and master-data management.

## 4. Strategic alignment

The project supports BerlinRetail GmbH’s objectives to modernize operations, improve decision-making, strengthen process controls and create a scalable digital foundation for future growth.

## 5. Expected benefits

| Benefit | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Faster reporting | Monthly reporting cycle time | Reduce by 40% |
| Fewer manual approvals | Manual approval steps | Reduce by 30% |
| Improved data quality | Critical master-data errors | Reduce by 50% |
| Better visibility | Departments using standard dashboards | 100% of core functions |
| Process standardization | Core processes migrated to workflow platform | 80% |

## 6. Indicative costs

| Cost category | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Software licences and cloud platform | €420,000 |
| Implementation partner | €480,000 |
| Internal project team and backfill | €180,000 |
| Data migration and integration | €80,000 |
| Training and change management | €40,000 |
| Contingency | €40,000 |
| **Total** | **€1,240,000** |

*Note: The €1.24 million planning estimate will be reconciled to the €1.2 million approved budget during baseline development.*

## 7. Indicative timeline

| Phase | Duration |
|---|---:|
| Initiation and mobilization | Month 1 |
| Detailed design and vendor onboarding | Months 2–3 |
| Configuration and integration | Months 4–7 |
| Data migration and testing | Months 8–10 |
| Training and deployment | Month 11 |

## 8. Key risks

- Vendor delivery delay affecting the critical path.
- Incomplete or poor-quality legacy data.
- Limited user adoption without effective training and change management.
- Integration complexity with existing systems.
- Scope growth from department-specific requirements.

## 9. Recommendation

Approve initiation of the Enterprise Digital Transformation Project, appoint the project sponsor and project manager, establish the steering committee, and authorize development of the project charter, baseline schedule, budget and risk register.
