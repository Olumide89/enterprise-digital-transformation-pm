# RAID Log — Enterprise Digital Transformation

**R = Risk, A = Assumption, I = Issue, D = Dependency**

| ID | Type | Description | Impact | Probability | Owner | Response / action | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Risk | Vendor resources are delayed, affecting configuration and integration | High | Medium | Project Manager | Weekly vendor capacity review; identify backup resources; escalate if slippage exceeds two weeks | Open |
| R-02 | Risk | Legacy data quality is insufficient for migration | High | High | Data Lead | Early data profiling; cleansing plan; department data owners accountable for validation | Open |
| R-03 | Risk | Users do not adopt the new workflows | High | Medium | Change Lead | Change impact assessment, key-user network, role-based training and adoption KPIs | Open |
| R-04 | Risk | Integration complexity causes testing delays | Medium | Medium | Technical Lead | Early interface design, integration test environment and defect triage | Open |
| R-05 | Risk | Scope grows through department-specific requests | Medium | High | Project Manager | Scope statement, change-control process and steering-committee approval | Open |
| A-01 | Assumption | Key users are available for design, testing and training | High | — | Sponsor | Confirm availability in resource plan | Open |
| A-02 | Assumption | Vendor can mobilize by the end of Month 2 | High | — | Vendor Manager | Contract milestones and onboarding checklist | Open |
| I-01 | Issue | Reporting requirements differ across departments | Medium | — | Business Analyst | Facilitate standard reporting workshop; document agreed core reports | Open |
| D-01 | Dependency | Finance sign-off is required before finance workflow configuration | High | — | Finance Director | Schedule sign-off workshop in Month 3 | Open |
| D-02 | Dependency | IT security approval is required before production deployment | High | — | IT Director | Submit security assessment during testing phase | Open |
